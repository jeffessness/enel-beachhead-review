## The Channel Doctrine, Argued

The doctrine, from the memo of 28 July 2026: no paid social, no influencer partnering or sponsorship, on the grounds that Ēnel elected to displace influencers and that paid social is where influencers operate. The financial model follows it and carries no influencer partnering anywhere. Influencers appear only as study subjects. Growth is organic through ambassadors, events, credentialed speakers, and participants reposting on their own accounts, with paid spend phasing to zero and acquisition cost gliding from about $7 a person to $0.13.

### Where the doctrine and the opening agree, which is the finding that matters most

The doctrine's standing weakness is that ruling out bought attention leaves organic reach carrying almost all of growth, with ambassadors as the named mechanism, and ambassadors are slow, local and unproven at this stage of the company. Act one supplies the channel the doctrine has always been missing. A federal agency stated in writing in July 2026 that the evidence to evaluate these compounds does not exist, its own advisory committee overruled it on six of seven, and a public rulemaking is now open with no evidence in it. That is earned press on a beat the trade and general press already cover, and it costs no media budget.

**The doctrine needs act one more than act one needs the doctrine.** Any version of this plan that drops the peptide registry leaves the channel constraint carrying weight it has never been asked to carry in public.

### Collision one · The process mark

Act three issues a creator something to display in exchange for participating. No money moves and nothing is paid against outcome, so it satisfies the letter of the doctrine. It may not satisfy its purpose, because an Ēnel mark on a creator's account is Ēnel appearing in paid-social-shaped inventory by another route, carried by somebody with every commercial incentive to imply more than the finding supports.

**Resolution offered.** Issue no artwork. A creator may state a fact about themselves, that they were studied by Ēnel and that the finding is published at a named link. A logo is inventory. A sentence and a link is a citation. That satisfies the purpose of the doctrine, preserves the checkability that makes the arrangement worth anything, and is consistent with the standing decision to defer mark licensing until the mark carries three to five years of credibility. **The principal decides, on a one-page reading filed by James Christopher.**

### Collision two · Recruitment channel

Act one recruits from peptide forums, Discords and subreddits, which police promotional posting. The doctrine rules out paid channels, so community recruitment is the only route the doctrine leaves, and it is the route most likely to produce a moderator ban and a poor first impression with the population act one needs. Nobody owns it and nobody has asked whether permission is obtainable.

**Resolution offered.** Two routes, and only one of them needs anybody's permission. Moderator permission is the cheap test and should be run this week. The channel that does not need permission already exists: US peptide searches ran at roughly 10.1 million a month in January 2026 and the two peptide front doors are live and answer the question a searcher arrived with. Recruitment by being the answer to the search is fully inside the doctrine and requires no gatekeeper. **Forum permission is the test. Search is the channel.**

### Collision three · What the consumer line is for

The prior record assigned a kill signal for the consumer revenue line by 4 September. If peptides recruit rather than monetise on the consumer surface, the metric behind that signal changes from consumer revenue per member to enrolment yield per member, and a threshold written this month against the wrong metric would kill or spare the wrong thing.

**Resolution offered.** The date holds and the metric changes. Enrolments per hundred registered members per month, one threshold, written before the number is known.
